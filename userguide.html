BYD Fuel Calculator — User Guide

Built for aftermarket fuel tank conversions

When you replace the factory fuel tank in a BYD vehicle with an aftermarket or larger capacity tank, the original fuel sender unit stays in place — but it was calibrated for the factory tank's shape and volume. This means your dashboard km range, BYD app percentage, and fuel gauge are now all reading against the wrong reference — they no longer accurately reflect how much fuel is actually in your new tank.

This app solves that problem. By letting you enter your own calibration data — what the sender reads at each known fuel level in your new tank — the app rebuilds its calculation to match your specific setup. The result is an accurate, real-world litre reading from whatever your dashboard or BYD app is showing.

The core idea: You tell the app what your instruments show when you have 10L, 20L, 30L... in the tank. The app learns your setup and converts any future reading into actual litres — automatically.
Why the fuel sender lies

The fuel sender unit is a float on an arm inside the tank. As the fuel level drops, the float drops with it, sending a varying resistance signal to the dashboard and ECU. The factory calibration maps that resistance range to a percentage and a km range — but only for the factory tank's exact shape and volume.

When you fit an aftermarket tank — especially a larger one — the float arm travels through the same resistance range but now represents a completely different volume of fuel. A reading of 50% on the factory calibration might mean 40L in a factory tank but 65L in a larger aftermarket tank.

The BYD app and dashboard have no way to know the tank has changed. They keep applying the factory calibration. This app gives you a way to apply the correct one.

⚠ Never rely solely on the factory dashboard reading after a tank swap. Until you have entered your own calibration data into this app, treat the litre reading as an estimate only.
Calibrating for your tank — the key step

Calibration is the process of teaching the app what your fuel sender reads at each known fuel level. The more data points you collect, the more accurate the app becomes. You don't need all eight rows filled in straight away — even three or four good data points will give a useful result.

The best time to collect data is when filling up. Each time you add a measured amount of fuel, note what the dashboard km range and BYD app percentage show immediately after.

💡 The most practical approach: start with an empty tank, add exactly 10L, and note the readings. Add another 10L, note again. Repeat until full. You will have a complete calibration table in one fill-up session.
1
Open Settings — Calibration Data
Tap the Settings button at the bottom of the main screen. The calibration panel will expand below.
2
Fill in the Dashboard km range table
For each litre level shown in the left column (10L, 20L, 30L...), enter what km range your dashboard was showing when your tank held that amount of fuel. Leave rows blank if you don't have data for that level.
3
Fill in the BYD App % table
Do the same for the BYD app percentage readings. You can fill in one table or both — use whichever reading you prefer to enter day-to-day.
4
Tap Save & apply
The app immediately rebuilds its calibration algorithm from your data. All future readings will now be calculated against your specific tank. Your calibration is saved and remembered between sessions.
5
Refine over time
As you collect more data points, come back and fill in additional rows. The more complete your calibration table, the more accurate the litre readings will be across the full range of your tank.
⚠ The greyed-out default values in the calibration table were measured by the app developer after fitting a Brown Davis 120L long range tank to his own BYD. They are a real-world starting point, but your tank installation may behave differently. Replace them with your own measured data as soon as you are able to for the most accurate results.
Installing on your iPhone
1
Open in Safari
Open the app URL in Safari on your iPhone. The install option is only available in Safari — not Chrome or other browsers on iOS.
2
Tap the Share button
Tap the Share icon at the bottom of the Safari screen — it looks like a box with an arrow pointing upward.
3
Add to Home Screen
Scroll down in the share sheet and tap Add to Home Screen. Give it a name and tap Add.
4
Launch like a native app
The app icon appears on your home screen. It opens fullscreen without browser controls and works offline — your calibration data is stored on your device.
Day-to-day use

Once calibrated, using the app takes a few seconds. Select your input mode — Dashboard km range or BYD App % — and enter the reading from your car. The app instantly shows you actual litres remaining, litres to fill up, and estimated distance remaining.

Both input modes are linked. If you enter a km reading and then switch to the % tab, it will show the equivalent percentage — and vice versa. Use whichever reading is easiest to see at the time.

The app remembers your last reading and input mode between sessions, so it opens right where you left off.

Manual litre override

If you know exactly how many litres are in the tank — for example, you've just added a precisely measured amount at the bowser — tap the large green litre number and type the known value directly.

An amber ✎ manual override badge appears to show the app is using your entered value. This is especially useful during the calibration process when you are filling to exact measured amounts. To return to normal mode, simply move either slider.

Colour guide
Green
More than 30L remaining — comfortable range.
Orange
10–20L remaining — getting low, plan to fill up soon.
Red
10L or less remaining — fill up now.

Colour transitions are gradual — the gauge and litre number blend smoothly between red, orange, and green as the level changes, so you can see the trend before it becomes critical. An alert message also appears at 20L and below.

Distance remaining

Set your vehicle's average fuel consumption using the fuel burn rate slider at the bottom of the input card (in L/100km, default 12.0). The app calculates estimated distance remaining as:

distance = (litres ÷ burn rate) × 100

Your burn rate setting is saved between sessions. Adjust it to match your driving style — highway driving will typically give a lower burn rate and longer range than city driving.

⚠ Distance remaining is an estimate. Actual range varies with speed, load, terrain, and air conditioning use. Always maintain a safe fuel reserve — do not run a freshly converted tank to empty until you have a well-established calibration.
Sharing with other BYD owners

Tap Share this app at the bottom of the main screen to open the iOS share sheet. Send the link via Messages, WhatsApp, email, or AirDrop.

Each user can enter their own calibration data for their specific tank setup — the app is fully independent per device. If you have friends who have done the same aftermarket tank conversion, sharing your calibration readings with them can give them a useful starting point before they collect their own data.
